## Overview

Cellular automata are technically machines composed of blocks. Each
block has a set of rules for how it interprets its surrounding
environment. Every tick of the clock, or every "step", each block
updates its state to a new state based on its surrounding environment.
That is, it reads its surrounding and updates its value to a new value
based on its surroundings.

For a 1D automaton, its surrounding is 2 blocks (if you imagine a tape
composed of blocks).

So the "center block" reads its surrounding "neighbors", and figures out
its next value. That's it.

In our 2D CA's, green is the "off" state, and purple is the "on" state.
Or you could think about it as green = 0, purple = 1, or green = dead,
purple = alive, whatever, it is just 2 states.

A simple cellular automaton (CA) is one where each block has the same
set of rules for determining state, and each block can only be in 2
possible states. We represent the "on" state as purple, and "off" as
green, in a simple 2-state CA.

We can then generate all kinds of evolving patterns given this state.

## Why Cellular Automata are interesting

CA's are interesting in part because
[the universe may be one big cellular automaton](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.1548)!

Try and imagine how these simple blocks are simply reading the current
neighbor state and updating their own state, as a metaphor for life. We
read our senses and write to our experience how we feel, at every
moment. Our feelings are a trace/record of the sense information we
received. In the simple case, we can feel good or bad (purple or green),
or good, neutral, and bad (purple, yellow, green). If every block is a
"self", and every block feels these 2 or 3 feelings, then you can
imagine every block as a "self" organized into a 2D grid!

Now try and imagine a 3D grid doing this! This could be like 3D space,
with souls in it.

Now try and imagine somehow that the evolving pattern isn't just
"random", but it is like how life evolved out of nothing. It gets
organized, forms complex systems nested inside the overall grid, etc..
These systems interact with each other, forming other systems, like
planets, or biological cells. These then cause further systems to evolve
and interact, all cellular automata. Something like that!

Just try and imagine how that might work, looking at this simple 2D demo
CAs as a metaphor into the larger more complex system that the universe
is.
