Math Notes

This work aims to cover at a high level the major areas of mathematics and the key concepts within them, so as to provide you with an overview of what kinds of things are considered in mathematics. It will introduce you to all the things you might need to know to begin reading research papers heavy in math in all sorts of disciplines. While we strive to cover as much as possible, this work is by no means complete, as the field of mathematics is both vast and deep. Instead you should come out on the other side knowing most of the foundational concepts across a wide variety of fields, upon which you can build as you read more in specific areas of interest.

Clifford algebra

New research into spacetime modeling.

Algebraic representation of geometry. According to [https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.3619.pdf], Clifford’s geometric algebra (GA) provides the most elegant description of physical space. It provides an improved description of two key physical phenomena, electromagnetism and quantum theory, without using tensors or complex vector spaces.

In order to form a unified algebra of 3D space we need to integrate the algebra of the complex numbers and quaternions within the framework of Cartesian coordinates. This was achieved by Clifford in 1873, who named his system, Geometric Algebra (GA).
