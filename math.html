Math Notes

This work aims to cover at a high level the major areas of mathematics and the key concepts within them, so as to provide you with an overview of what kinds of things are considered in mathematics. It will introduce you to all the things you might need to know to begin reading research papers heavy in math in all sorts of disciplines. While we strive to cover as much as possible, this work is by no means complete, as the field of mathematics is both vast and deep. Instead you should come out on the other side knowing most of the foundational concepts across a wide variety of fields, upon which you can build as you read more in specific areas of interest.

Mathematics

This first section discusses how to think about mathematics generally, covering how to read the symbols, how to think about proofs, and how to think about modeling at a high level.

First, papers typically give "definitions" where they use a new term and provide the mathematical symbols for that term. Then after creating many definitions, they elaborate on those definitions in the form of theorems or lemmas or corollaries, providing proofs for them which transform the theorem into the axioms or definitions. So your first goal is to understand the definitions and the symbols used in the definitions. This in itself is already a difficult task because it is not as formalized as programming language source code, and instead relies on arbitrary natural language mixed in with mathematical symbols. So write these definitions down, or transform them into a new form that makes it easier for you to memorize or quickly recall. This way when you read the rest of the paper (and other papers) you can easily continue where you left off and build off your knowledge. Without first clearly learning the definitions, the impact of the theorems might not be immediately obvious, you might overlook some important insight.
