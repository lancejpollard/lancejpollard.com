
The moon observes from its perch, high above the peak,
Telling other creatures, their task is never complete.
Far beneath the mountain a valley is carved,
As a river churns for endless years, through stone so hard.

7 Rabbit approaches like a hunter the infinite byte
Lighting the way for others to build into the night,
The valley, the breeze, the animals, the trees,
All working together, opening our eyes to see.

7 Rabbit basks in the warm sun and breathes the mountain air.
Learns from so many, working together is all that’s fair.
All that we call nature is constantly changing.
Each day, each hour, every minute rearranging.

New paths taken as friends come along,
Learning the outcomes in nature are rarely wrong.
7 Rabbit is there to advise and to learn,
Making sure progress survives every turn.
